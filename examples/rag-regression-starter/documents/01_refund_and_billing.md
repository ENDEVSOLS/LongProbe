# Synthetic SaaS Policy — Refund and Billing Policy

> **Source & Reuse Rights:** This document is 100% synthetic SaaS policy documentation created by EnDevSols for public testing under the MIT License. It contains no customer data, PII, or confidential credentials.

## 1. Subscription & Refund Windows
- **Annual Subscriptions:** Annual subscriptions are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of initial purchase. After this 30-day window, subscriptions are non-refundable for the elapsed period.
- **Prorated Refunds:** Prorated refunds are available after 30 days upon written request to billing support, calculated on remaining whole unused months.
- **Monthly Subscriptions:** Monthly plans may be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the current billing cycle without prorated refunds.

## 2. Invoicing & Payment Terms
- **Enterprise Payment Terms:** Standard enterprise invoice payment terms are net 30 days from date of issuance.
- **Early Payment Discount:** Enterprise clients receive a 2% early payment discount for settling invoices within 10 days.
- **Overdue Invoices:** A 1.5% monthly late fee applies to any past due balance exceeding 30 calendar days.
