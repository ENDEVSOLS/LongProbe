## 1. Availability Guarantees
- **Monthly Uptime SLA:** Enterprise tier subscriptions include a guaranteed 99.95% monthly uptime service level agreement.
- **Incident Response Targets:** Severity 1 critical incidents receive an initial engineering response within 15 minutes 24/7.
- **SLA Penalty Credits:** Customers receive a 10% monthly service credit if monthly uptime drops below 99.9%.

## 2. Maintenance & Continuity
- **Scheduled Maintenance Notice:** Scheduled system maintenance requires a minimum of 5 business days advance notification.
- **Disaster Recovery Objectives:** The disaster recovery RPO for all primary production databases is guaranteed under 15 minutes.
